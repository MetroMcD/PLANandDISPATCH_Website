AI Features

The AI handles dispatching. You stay in control.

No buzzword overkill — every feature saves real work, every day.

Auto-Dispatch

The inbox dispatches itself: the AI reads every task, matches it against team skill profiles and assigns it to the right person — including a capacity check. If the top candidate is fully booked, it picks the runner-up or flags the task for manual planning.

AI classification

An unstructured ticket becomes a structured task: summary, priority, task type, next step.

Effort estimation

The AI estimates each task using a self-maintained reference table calibrated to your own experience — manually set values are never overwritten.

Milestone mapping

New tasks are automatically assigned to the matching project milestone — a phase model inspired by PRINCE2, stored as an editable template.

AI weekly briefing

One click delivers the management briefing: overdue items, critical issues, per-person capacity and concrete recommendations.

Billing texts & email assistant

Type keywords into time tracking — the AI turns them into clean, billable service descriptions. Customer status emails are pre-drafted and opened in your own mail client.

Your AI, your rules.

PLAN and DISPATCH works with Anthropic (Claude), OpenAI or fully local AI servers (LM Studio, Ollama, vLLM and more) inside your own network. No data ever has to leave the building. And if the AI is unreachable, the app simply keeps working without it.
