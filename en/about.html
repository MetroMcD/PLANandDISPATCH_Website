About me

René Münz — project manager and developer of PLAN and DISPATCH AI.

My background is in project management — not the theory, but the engine room. As a project lead I have guided interdisciplinary teams through software rollouts, system migrations and process changes: with budget responsibility, milestone tracking and the constant balancing act between management, departments and external service providers.

What I learned along the way: good projects rarely fail because of the technology. They fail because of a lack of overview, prioritisation and communication.

Why Plan and Dispatch AI

In recent years one topic has taken hold of me more and more: the combination of project management and AI. dispatcher-ai grew out of my own initiative — not a client project, but a tool I built myself because I would have needed it myself. It bundles what actually makes day-to-day project work easier, and uses AI where it delivers real value instead of just being a buzzword.

Technically I move between classic project-management craft and modern AI tools — from Claude Code and Codex through to locally hosted language models (Ollama, LM Studio).
